# agforge (retired)

**This instance is retired since 2026-10-14 and answers nothing.** Its
listener and its request service are stopped; posts in `{instance}`,
`assetplan-` and `assetrun-` topics are no longer read.

Media assets — images, video, music, speech, text — are now made by the
**forge** project of the workflow editor:

- ask in the Zulip channel `wf-forge.provide` (a `request-…` topic), or with
  *Request a run* on the forge project in the workflow editor;
- `wf-forge.develop-capability` is where unmet demand is turned into new
  capability, and `wf-forge.freework` takes anything else.

Earlier requests and their conversations stay as they are. A delivered
object's key (`[S3KEY] …`) still names a stored file; forge's tools make a
fresh link from it (`tools/forge asset url <key>` in the forge project).
